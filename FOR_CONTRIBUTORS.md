## Why I dropped this project

Initially, I thought that I could create this game all by myself, but I underestimated the time and effort needed.
For reference, while I really enjoyed programming, drawing art was an absolutely terrible experience—I'm not an artist.
As a solo developer, I couldn't neglect one aspect and pour all of my effort into the other.
When I started drawing locations, I knew that I should stop this mess, or it would've taken me a year just to draw all of them.
There were a lot of ideas I wanted to implement, but I just didn't have enough time and energy to bring them to life,
such as Wi-Fi trading, Wi-Fi battles, and modules (like Balatro jokers).


## How you can help

If you are a C++ or Python developer, an artist, a translator, or a writer, you can contribute to this project.
There's a lot of work left and a lot of bugs to fix.


## Contribution Rules

Do not use AI.

### For artists, writers, and translators

Try to keep it in one style, and keep it civil.
Remember that this is a *Pokémon Black* fan game, so the plot should be based on *Pokémon Black*.
Don't try to make a completely different story, characters, or Pokémon.

### For developers

Read `DEVELOPMENT_RULES.md`.

This project is very important to me, so if you don't know what you are doing, please don't touch anything.
